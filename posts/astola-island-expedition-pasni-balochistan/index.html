Astola Island Expedition: The Untamed 'Island of the Seven Hills' off the Pasni Coast

Pakistan's largest offshore island features pristine turquoise coral waters, endangered sea turtles, and dramatic limestone cliffs in the remote northern Arabian Sea.

Bilal Mengal Coastal & Marine Expedition Leader
• 07 Oct 2026
6.7 km² ISOLATED MARINE WILDLIFE RESERVE

Rising majestically from the emerald waters of the Arabian Sea some 39 kilometers south of Pasni, Astola Island ('Jezira Haft Talar' or Island of the Seven Hills) is Pakistan's first designated Marine Protected Area (MPA). Stretching 6.7 square kilometers with sheer limestone sea cliffs towering over white sand coves, Astola remains one of South Asia's most pristine and remote marine frontiers.

Because Astola has zero permanent human inhabitants, no fresh groundwater, and no mobile connectivity, visiting the island is a genuine wilderness expedition. Journeying by traditional wooden fishing trawlers or high-speed twin-engine launches from Pasni harbor takes between 2 to 4 hours across open pelagic waters where pods of Indo-Pacific humpback dolphins frequently escort passing boats.

Beneath the water's surface, Astola is a sanctuary for marine biodiversity. Vibrant hard coral reefs shelter green sea turtles, hawksbill turtles, moray eels, and schools of barracuda. On land, the island is the exclusive habitat of the endemic Astola viper (Echis carinatus astolae) and serves as a vital nesting refuge for migratory sooty gulls and bridled terns.

Navigating Pasni Harbor to the Cliffs of Haft Talar

Expeditions launch from Pasni's historic fish harbor during calm tidal windows between October and March. Cruising south past sea stacks, the island appears on the horizon as an imposing fortress flanked by deep sapphire waters.

Underwater Reefs, Scuba Diving & Sea Turtle Sanctuaries

With underwater visibility exceeding 15 meters during winter, Astola's coral lagoons offer Pakistan's premier scuba diving and snorkeling terrain. Sea caves on the southern flank provide safe shelter for endangered marine life.

Strict Wilderness Camping & Conservation Protocols

Visitors must bring all fresh water, food, shade shelters, and first aid supplies from the mainland. The Balochistan Wildlife Department enforces strict waste removal mandates to prevent plastic pollution on turtle nesting beaches.

⚡

Executive Takeaways & Key Insights

  • Pakistan's first Marine Protected Area spanning 6.7 square kilometers of untouched coral reef habitats.
  • Prime diving and expedition season runs from November to February with placid sea conditions.
  • Demands 100% self-sufficient wilderness camping and mandatory mainland boat charter logistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there fresh water or electricity on Astola Island?

No. The island is completely uninhabited without fresh water springs or commercial power. Campers must carry at least 4 to 5 liters of potable water per person per day from Pasni.

How do you reach Pasni to catch the boat to Astola?

Pasni is accessible via the Makran Coastal Highway (N-10), located approximately 6 hours drive west of Karachi or 1.5 hours east of Gwadar international airport.
