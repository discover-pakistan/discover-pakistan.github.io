The Broghil Valley & Karambar Lake: Traversing the Remote High Roof of Upper Chitral

Bordering Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor, this high-altitude expedition crosses peat bogs, turquoise glacial lakes, and traditional Wakhi yak pastures at 4,300 meters.

Dr. Tariq Chitrali High Hindu Kush Alpine Guide
• 07 Oct 2026
4,300m KARAMBAR LAKE ELEVATION

Located at the extreme northern apex of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa where Pakistan meets the narrow finger of Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor, Broghil Valley remains one of the most secluded and culturally distinct frontiers on the planet. Flanked by glaciated Hindu Kush massifs, Broghil is home to the semi-nomadic Wakhi people who herd domestic yaks across wide sub-alpine peat bogs and glacial valleys.

The crowning jewel of any expedition into Broghil is Karambar Lake (also known as Qurumbar Lake), recognized as the 31st highest lake in the world at an altitude of 4,300 meters. Spanning over 3.9 square kilometers with a depth exceeding 55 meters, the crystal-clear turquoise waters reflect snow-dusted ridges in absolute silence, untamed by roads or commercial resorts.

Reaching Karambar requires a rugged multi-day trek starting from the 4x4 jeep terminus at Lashkargaz. Trekkers journey across wide grassy plains, cross glacial rivers, and camp beneath starlit skies where light pollution is non-existent. Each July, the valley hosts the renowned Broghil Festival, where Wakhi horsemen compete in traditional polo, yak races, and buzkashi.

Journey to the Edge of the Wakhan Corridor

From Chitral town, the route follows the Mastuj River through Yarkhun valley via 4x4 mountain tracks carved into sheer rock faces before ascending onto the wide plateau of Broghil National Park.

Karambar Lake: The Sapphire Jewel of the High Hindu Kush

Karambar Lake sits in a biologically pristine glacial bowl supporting rare high-altitude trout and migratory waterbirds. Standing along its southern shore, the lake connects directly into the Karambar Pass leading into Ishkoman, Gilgit-Baltistan.

Wakhi Yak Pastoralism and the Annual Broghil Festival

The Wakhi inhabitants have adapted to severe winters lasting seven months. Their stone homes, woolen yurts, and fermented yak dairy traditions offer a window into an ancient Central Asian way of life.

Executive Takeaways & Key Insights

  • Karambar Lake at 4,300m is among the highest, largest alpine lakes in the Hindu Kush range.
  • Offers an unvarnished encounter with indigenous Wakhi yak herders along the Afghan border.
  • Trekking window is strictly limited to July through mid-September before heavy winter snowfall closes passes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Karambar Lake be reached by jeep or motor vehicle?

No. The 4x4 jeep track terminates at Lashkargaz. The final 22 kilometers to Karambar Lake must be completed on foot or via hired pack horses over 1 to 2 days of trekking.

Do Pakistani tourists need security clearance to visit Broghil?

Pakistani citizens require only valid original CNICs registered at local police and Chitral Levies checkpoints. Foreign travelers require security NOC permits from the Ministry of Interior.
