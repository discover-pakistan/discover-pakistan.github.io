The New 66-km Astore to Skardu Alpine Highway: Revolutionizing Overland Access to Deosai Plains

Newly completed strategic mountain road bypasses rugged river gorges, slashing transit times and unlocking unprecedented autumn tourism circuits.

Kamran Alvi Senior Alpine Expedition Guide
• 08 Oct 2026
66 KM ALPINE CORRIDOR

In one of the most consequential infrastructure achievements for Northern Pakistan's tourism geography, the newly paved 66-kilometer Astore-to-Skardu alpine highway has officially opened for regular light passenger traffic.

Historically, navigating between the fertile Astore Valley, the legendary Deosai National Park plateau, and the Baltistan capital of Skardu required grueling eight-hour off-road journeys across precarious unpaved tracks. The engineered highway reduces travel time to under 2.5 hours on all-weather tarmac.

Mountaineers, landscape photographers, and eco-travelers now have seamless connectivity to witness the world's second-highest alpine plateau without demanding specialist expedition vehicles, unlocking substantial economic prosperity for high-altitude mountain communities.

Engineering Through High-Altitude Glacial Permafrost

Engineers deployed modern retaining walls, concrete avalanche sheds, and culverts engineered to withstand glacial melt runoff and winter sub-zero freeze-thaw cycles above 3,500 meters.

A Boost for Community-Based Ecotourism

Local homestays, licensed high-altitude porters, and trout hatcheries in Gorikot and Chilam are anticipating record seasonal visitor inflows while preserving Deosai's fragile brown bear habitat.

⚡

Executive Takeaways & Key Insights

  • 66-km Astore-Skardu highway slashes transit time from 8 hours to under 2.5 hours.
  • Provides safe, all-weather paved access connecting Astore, Deosai Plains, and Baltistan.
  • Spurs sustainable ecotourism revenue for isolated alpine villages while expanding Northern travel circuits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the new Astore-Skardu road accessible in standard saloon cars?

Yes, during late spring through mid-autumn, the engineered asphalt allows smooth passage for conventional passenger cars and mini-vans.

What attractions are along this highway?

Rama Lake, Chilam Valley gateway, Deosai National Park, Sheosar Lake, and the majestic Sadpara Lake before entering Skardu.
